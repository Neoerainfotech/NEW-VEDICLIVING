About us

Sree Jnanambika Research Foundation for Vedicliving

Sree Jnanambika Research Foundation for Vedic Living is working for the teaching of Vedas and spreading Sanathana Dharma worldwide for peace, prosperity, and unity of the human race. A group of Vedic academics who have completed Prayoga and Veda adhyayanam, along with the Sree Jnanambika Research Foundation for Vedic Living, launched Vedicliving.in. We hope to educate people worldwide about our magnificent Dharma through www.vedicliving.in.
As the Vedic Rishis formulated it, the mere possession of knowledge is meaningless without practical application. We realized that for everyone to achieve both spiritual and material advancement, we must uphold their teachings by performing the Vaideeka Prayogas—Shodasha Samaskara, Homams, Santhi, Pariharas, and Poojas—correctly. For that, we've set up all the required facilities, ensuring they align with the original instructions of the ancient Vedic Seers.
Most of the problems people face today—financial, health, divorce, and psychological—stem from the lack of daily prescribed rituals (Nithyakarmanushtana) and incorrectly performed rites (Shodasha Samaskaras and Shanthis). These can be resolved through proper adherence to Vedic Prayogas and Anushtanas. We advise doing Pariharas based on Shastra Pramana (scriptural authority), as suggested on our site. Indian astrology is used only as a support system where necessary, not as the primary method.
